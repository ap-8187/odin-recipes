Green scrambled eggs

Description of recipe

Zazz up your scrambled egg routine with this colorful and healthy spin on scrambled eggs. Spinach adds a daily dose of vegetables while the goat cheese gives a satisfying tang.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup frozen spinach
  • 1/2 cup goat cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Procedure

  1. Crack the eggs into a small bowl and scramble with a fork.
  2. Add spinach and mix.
  3. Heat skillet to medium heat.
  4. Pour egg mixture into pan.
  5. Crumble goat cheese onto cooking eggs.
  6. Cook for 2 minutes. Then, flip one half of the omelette over onto itself.
  7. Cook each side another minute. Then, serve.
