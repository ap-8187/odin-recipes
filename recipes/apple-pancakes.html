Apple pancakes

Description of recipe

This is a deliciously simple pancake recipe that you can whip up anytime. The addition of apples and cinnamon adds the best fall flavor to a breakfast favorite.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup apple sauce
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1 tblsp maple syrup
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Procedure

  1. In a medium bowl combine all the wet ingredients.
  2. In another medium bowl, combine the dry ingredients.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and mix together.
  4. Let batter sit for several minutes while you heat up a skillet to medium high heat.
  5. When ready to cook, pour quarter cup amounts onto heated skillet. Cook for 5 minutes. Then, turn over and cook another 2 minutes.
  6. Remove from heat. Read to eat immediately!
