Supreme grilled cheese

Description of recipe

This is not your traditional grilled cheese sandwich! Instead, this is the supreme of all grilled sandwiches!

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • Butter
  • 2 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 tblsp pickle relish
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Procedure

  1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Slice tomato into thin slices.
  3. Heat a skillet to medium.
  4. Place one slice of bread butter side down on skillet.
  5. Place both slices of cheese on top of the gilling bread.
  6. Add sliced tomato and pickle relish.
  7. Place 2nd slice of bread onto the cooking sandwich, butter side up.
  8. Cook for 5 minutes. Then, flip sandwich over.
  9. Cook the sandwich for another 5 mins until golden brown. Then, serve.
